白頭菜肉圓
Baitou Meat Dumpling
The Vibe
Before heading to the coast, we stop for one of Taichung's most iconic street snacks. 白頭菜肉圓 — crispy on the outside, soft and savory within — is a style of deep-fried pork dumpling that locals have lined up for generations. A small, perfect taste of everyday Taiwanese life.
Highlights
  • A Taichung classic — deep-fried rice dumplings filled with seasoned pork and bamboo shoots.
  • Crispy golden shell with a chewy, tender interior — unlike anything you'd find back home.
  • The perfect fuel before an afternoon walk on the wetlands.
2
14:45 – 17:30  ·  Stop Two
Gaomei Wetlands
高美濕地
The Vibe
One of Taiwan's most magical landscapes. As the afternoon light softens toward golden hour, the shallow waters of Gaomei reflect the sky and the silhouettes of giant wind turbines standing at the edge of the Taiwan Strait. Visitors wade barefoot through the warm, shallow flats as the sun descends — a quiet, almost surreal experience that stays with you long after you leave.
Highlights
  • Wade barefoot through the shallow wetland flats — warm, calm, and unlike any beach experience.
  • Iconic wind turbines lining the coast, dramatic against the setting sun.
  • One of Taiwan's best sunset spots — golden hour here is genuinely spectacular on a clear day.
  • Rich birdlife and coastal ecosystem, protected as a nature reserve.
  • Tip: bring sandals or flip-flops you don't mind getting wet, and a change of socks!

14:30 – 15:15  ·  Rainy Day Stop A
Chung Hsing Cambridge
& Chung Hsing Lake
The Vibe
National Chung Hsing University's campus is anchored by a shimmering lake that locals call their weekend sanctuary. The famous "Black Forest" — a dense corridor of tropical trees — delivers a moment of quiet stillness. Even in light rain, the misty lakeside has its own gentle charm.
Highlights
  • Scenic lakeside promenade with swans and migratory waterfowl.
  • The "Black Forest" — a canopy of tropical hardwoods along the main campus avenue.
  • A glimpse into one of Taiwan's top research universities.
B
15:15 – 15:45  ·  Rainy Day Stop B
Authentic Taiwanese
Bubble Tea Experience
The Vibe
No visit to Taiwan is complete without the drink that changed how the world drinks tea. NCHU's campus bubble tea stand — 中興奶茶, open since 2018 — draws long queues for its freshly brewed black tea and chewy tapioca pearls. The perfect warm-up on a rainy afternoon.
Highlights
  • Boba was invented in Taiwan in the 1980s — this is the real thing.
  • A campus institution loved by generations of students.
  • Open daily 14:00–22:00.
C
15:45 – 16:30  ·  Rainy Day Stop C
Empire Sugar Factory
帝國製糖廠台中營業所
The Vibe
Built in the 1930s during Japan's colonial period, this beautifully preserved industrial site now houses creative studios, art installations, and indoor exhibitions. Rain only adds atmosphere to the weathered brick and iron — a living window into Taichung's agricultural history.
Highlights
  • Preserved Japanese colonial-era factory with indoor cultural exhibits.
  • Artisan vendors and creative spaces in the repurposed factory buildings.
  • The adjacent Springwater Park — artesian wells in the heart of the city.
D
16:45 – 17:45  ·  Rainy Day Stop D
Calligraphy Greenway
& Eslite Spectrum
The Vibe
Taichung's cultural heart. The Calligraphy Greenway runs through the city's most vibrant creative district, and on a rainy day, Eslite Spectrum — Taiwan's most beloved literary lifestyle bookstore — is the perfect place to wander. Design books, local crafts, specialty coffee, and a beautifully curated space that feels nothing like an ordinary bookshop.
Highlights
  • Eslite Spectrum (誠品書店) — a stunning indoor destination for books, design, and culture.
  • Independent boutiques and creative shops along the covered greenway corridor.
  • Excellent people-watching — Taichung's creative community on full display.
