Chung Hsing Cambridge
& Chung Hsing Lake
The Vibe
Step into a world where the pace of Taiwanese academic life unfolds in quiet, unhurried beauty. National Chung Hsing University (NCHU) is one of Taiwan's most prestigious research universities — and its sprawling campus is anchored by a shimmering lake that locals simply call their weekend sanctuary. The famous "Black Forest" — a dense corridor of towering tropical trees whose canopy knits together overhead — delivers a moment of pure stillness. On a Saturday afternoon, young families spread out on the lakeside grass, elderly couples feed the geese, and students drift by in relaxed contentment.
Highlights
  • A scenic promenade around Chung Hsing Lake, home to graceful swans and migratory waterfowl — perfect for unhurried conversation and photography.
  • The legendary "Black Forest" (黑森林) — a cathedral-like canopy of tropical hardwoods casting dramatic dappled light along the main campus avenue.
  • An immersive feel of Taiwan's top-ranked research institution; NCHU's colleges of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine are nationally celebrated.
  • Organic glimpses of local weekend life: families picnicking, students sketching, retirees practicing Tai Chi at the water's edge.

15:15 – 15:45  ·  Stop Two
Authentic Taiwanese
Bubble Tea Experience
The Vibe
No visit to Taiwan is complete without the drink that literally changed how the world drinks tea. The NCHU campus bubble tea stand — 中興奶茶, established in 2018 — is beloved among locals: the kind of spot that draws long weekend queues with zero advertising because the product speaks for itself. Freshly brewed black tea, hand-stirred with just the right measure of sweetness, and crowned with generously chewy tapioca pearls. This is boba in its most honest, unvarnished form.
Highlights
  • Taste the original: boba was invented in Taiwan in the 1980s and has since become one of the most influential global beverage trends of the modern era.
  • NCHU's signature milk tea is a campus institution — beloved by generations of students and a genuine local pilgrimage for foodies and travelers alike.
  • Open daily 14:00–22:00, perfectly timed as a refreshing mid-afternoon interlude before transitioning to Taichung's historic core.

Empire Sugar Factory
(帝國製糖廠台中營業所)
The Vibe
Standing before the Empire Sugar Factory is to stand at the crossroads of two eras. Built in the early 1930s during the Japanese colonial period, the original complex processed sugarcane harvested from the fertile Taichung plains. Today, its striking industrial bones have been reimagined as a living cultural canvas: art installations, creative studios, and weekend pop-up markets breathe new life into century-old brick and iron. Out front, the tranquil Springwater Park (湧泉公園), fed by natural artesian wells, offers a rare contrast — cool, clear water bubbling up beside weathered colonial architecture.
Highlights
  • One of Taichung's finest preserved Japanese colonial-era industrial landmarks — a rare architectural witness to the island's early 20th-century agricultural transformation.
  • The Springwater Park: a genuinely unusual natural feature in the heart of the city, where artesian groundwater creates a peaceful, park-like setting directly adjacent to the historic site.
  • Local creative vendors, artisan goods, and cultural exhibits now inhabit the factory's repurposed spaces — a seamless blend of history and contemporary creativity.
  • A photogenic landmark: the industrial patina of aged brick against lush green parkland and reflective springwater pools.

16:45 – 17:45  ·  Stop Four
Calligraphy Greenway
& Park Lane by CMP
The Vibe
If there is a single place that captures what modern Taichung aspires to be, it is here. The Calligraphy Greenway is a long, lush public corridor that traces a gentle arc through the city's cultural and commercial heart. Flanked by independent design shops, the celebrated Eslite Spectrum bookstore, open-air cafés, and rotating weekend artisan markets, it feels at once like a Parisian boulevard and a uniquely Taiwanese invention. On a weekend afternoon, the lawns fill with families, performers, and people who simply love being outside.
Highlights
  • Stroll the full length of the iconic greenway — a deliberate urban design project that transformed a former parking corridor into one of Asia's finest pedestrian cultural promenades.
  • Eslite Spectrum (誠品書店): Taiwan's most beloved literary lifestyle destination — a stunning, meticulously curated space where publishing, design, and culinary culture converge under one elegant roof.
  • Park Lane by CMP: a beautifully maintained open lawn where local creatives, weekend vendors, and curious visitors mingle in easy, convivial harmony.
  • Excellent people-watching and street photography — Taichung's creative community is in full display on Saturday afternoons.
